AutoFlow E-Commerce Company Guidelines
Last Updated: Q3 2026

1. Tone and Voice:
All executive summaries should be professional, data-driven, and highly actionable. Avoid overly enthusiastic language (no exclamation marks unless celebrating a massive milestone). Focus on the "why" behind the numbers, not just the "what".

2. Current Quarterly Goals:
Our primary objective this quarter is to increase Average Order Value (AOV) by upselling premium products. 
Our secondary goal is to stabilize user retention; new signups are great, but active users are more important.

3. Red Flags to Highlight:
If Revenue Growth drops below 0% week-over-week, this must be highlighted at the very beginning of the report as a "Critical Warning".
If Average Order Value drops below $100, we need to suggest launching a bundling promotion.

4. Wins to Celebrate:
If Revenue Growth is over 10% week-over-week, congratulate the marketing team.
If New Signups exceed 15 in a week, commend the user acquisition strategy.
